The problem

Students get tens of emails a day across two accounts. The one that matters sits between a sale and a newsletter.

How it works

Four steps from inbox to alert.

Setup takes about two minutes. After that, Surface runs on its own.

01 · connect

Link both inboxes

Sign in to your school account and your personal Gmail. Skip the second one if you only use one.

02 · rules

Name who matters

Add professors and bosses, and keep keywords like exam grade deadline cancelled switched on.

03 · summarize

Read it on your phone

Each important email gets a one-line summary, written on your phone. The email text never leaves it.

04 · alert

Get the buzz

A notification arrives right away. Tap it for the summary, or open the Important list to catch up.

Important! Email from your professor Cheat sheet allowed on Friday's exam.

Wrong call? Tap Not important or Mark important. Surface remembers the sender, and you can see and undo everything it learned in Settings.

Privacy

Your email stays on your phone.

Surface only reads mail to decide what matters. Here is exactly what it does and doesn't do.

Where data livesOn your phone. Only you can see it.
SummariesWritten on the device. Email text is never sent to an outside AI service.
School mailConnects through the Microsoft Graph API. Some schools require IT approval for outside apps.
Personal mailConnects through the Gmail API.
Sends or deletes mailNo. Surface reads and flags. Replying happens in your email app.
LearningSimple rules you can see: sender choices and keywords. Undo anything in Settings.
